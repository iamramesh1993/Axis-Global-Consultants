## Why students pick Australia

Good universities, generous post-study work rights, and a climate and lifestyle
that people genuinely want. Australia has also stayed relatively consistent on
graduate work rights while other countries have tightened, which matters if your
plan extends beyond the degree.

The trade-off is cost. Australia is the most expensive of the four destinations we
work with, both in tuition and in what it costs to make an application at all.

## What it actually costs

Tuition runs roughly AUD 22,000 to AUD 50,000 a year. Business and IT sit in the
middle. Engineering, health sciences and anything with lab or clinical placement
sit at the top.

For the visa you must evidence twelve months of living costs at AUD 29,710, plus
tuition for that period, plus travel. There is an income alternative: annual income
of AUD 60,000 or more from a parent or partner who is not travelling with you. In
practice most Pakistani families use the funds route, because documented income at
that level is harder to evidence to the Department's satisfaction than a
well-prepared bank position.

Then the visa charge itself: AUD 2,500, non-refundable, up from AUD 2,000 on
1 July 2026. That single number is why Australia rewards preparation and punishes
a speculative attempt.

## The Genuine Student requirement

This replaced the old Genuine Temporary Entrant test in March 2024, and it is the
part of an Australian application that most rewards honest work.

You will answer specific questions: your situation in Pakistan, why this course
and this provider over the options at home, what you understand about the cost and
conditions of living in Australia, and how the qualification connects to what you
intend to do next. Assessors read a great many of these. Generic answers, and
answers that plainly were not written by the student, read as exactly what they
are.

We help you write these properly, in your own voice. We do not write them for you,
because that is both dishonest and easy to spot.

## Applications and offers

Applications go directly to universities, most of which run rolling admissions.
You receive an offer, accept it, pay a deposit and your OSHC, and the provider
issues a Confirmation of Enrolment. The CoE is what you attach to the visa
application — you must hold a valid one when the decision is made.

Processing priority is governed by a Ministerial Direction, and applications lodged
on or after 14 November 2025 are assessed under Ministerial Direction 115. In plain
terms: your choice of provider affects how your application is queued.

## After you graduate

The Temporary Graduate visa gives post-study work rights, with length depending on
your qualification level. Its charge rose to AUD 5,750 on 1 July 2026, so factor
that into the full-journey budget rather than treating the student visa as the last
big payment.

## What we will tell you honestly

Australia is the destination where we most often advise students to wait a
semester. Not because the answer is no, but because a well-prepared February
application beats a rushed July one, and at AUD 2,500 a refusal is an expensive way
to learn that.
