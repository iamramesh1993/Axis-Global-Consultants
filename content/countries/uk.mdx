## Why students pick the UK

A taught master's in the UK is one year. That is the whole pitch, and it is a good
one. You finish a recognised postgraduate degree in twelve months instead of two,
which means one year of tuition and one year of living costs rather than two of
each. For a self-funding family in Pakistan, that arithmetic often decides it.

Undergraduate degrees run three years in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and
four in Scotland.

## What it actually costs

Tuition for international students sits roughly between £14,000 and £30,000 a
year. Business, humanities and social sciences cluster at the lower end.
Engineering, computing and anything lab-based sits higher. Clinical medicine and
dentistry are in a different bracket again and are effectively out of reach for
most self-funding students.

Add maintenance funds on top. The Home Office does not ask what you actually
spend — it asks you to prove a set monthly figure: £1,529 a month for London,
£1,171 a month elsewhere, for up to nine months. Those are visa thresholds, not
budgets. London in particular is difficult to live in on the minimum.

## The 28-day rule, plainly

This is where most Pakistani applications come apart, so read it twice.

The required amount must be in the account, at or above the required balance,
every day for 28 consecutive days. The closing balance date on your statement must
be within 31 days of the day you submit your visa application. If the balance dips
below the threshold on day nineteen, you start again from day one.

Money that arrives from a property sale, a committee, or several relatives at once
is not disqualified, but it does attract questions. Move funds early and leave them
alone.

## Applications and offers

Undergraduate applications go through UCAS. Postgraduate applications go directly
to each university, and most run rolling admissions — which means popular courses
close before the official deadline, not on it.

An offer is either conditional or unconditional. Once you accept an unconditional
offer and pay the required deposit, the university issues a CAS: a reference
number without which you cannot apply for the visa. A CAS is not permission to
enter the UK. It is one document in the visa application.

## After you graduate

The Graduate Route lets you stay and work without a sponsor. Two years for
bachelor's and master's graduates if you apply on or before 31 December 2026, and
18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027. Doctoral graduates get three
years regardless.

If you intend to stay on afterwards, the realistic path is a Skilled Worker visa,
which requires an employer willing to sponsor you and a salary at or above the
threshold for the role. Plenty of graduates manage it. Plenty do not. Anyone who
tells you a UK master's leads automatically to settlement is selling you something.
